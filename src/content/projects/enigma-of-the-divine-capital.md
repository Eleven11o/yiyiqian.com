experience: "The Enigma of the Divine Capital is a 25-minute stereoscopic 360° VR film developed through an industrial production pipeline. Yiyi wrote the full film and led the narrative and content production of one chapter, participating in historical research, narrative development, virtual production, testing, and final delivery."
context: "The production became a platform for two connected investigations: how a viewer can experientially confirm a non-human identity in non-interactive cinematic VR, and how historical architectural imagery can be translated into a large-scale stereoscopic 360° environment."
researchQuestion: "First, how can a viewer establish and internalize a cross-species identity in non-interactive cinematic VR when the body is invisible and agency is limited? Second, how can an architectural painting be translated into a large-scale stereoscopic 360° historical environment while preserving spatial legibility and depth?"
creativeProcess: "A first-person sequence in which the protagonist becomes a cat was developed as a design study in cross-species perceptual empathy. In parallel, the Tang-dynasty architectural painting Que Lou Yi Zhang Tu informed the construction of a large-scale historical environment through viewpoint translation, depth-based layering, and differentiated scene construction."
designProduction: "The team followed a complete industrial workflow from screenplay and historical research through virtual production, testing, and delivery. The film is 25 minutes long and produced in stereoscopic 3D, 8K, 360°, and 90 FPS."
researchMethod: "The Becoming a Cat study developed a three-layer identity-confirmation mechanism and used an exploratory user study. The environment-construction study used a practice-based case study to formulate a layered hybrid workflow for large urban VR scenes."
reflection: "The project connects film production and HCI research: specific narrative and environment-design problems become research questions that can be articulated, studied, and reported."
outcome: "The film has received official filing approval from the China Film Administration. The two investigations have produced a long-paper manuscript and a poster manuscript, both listed as under review in the current CV."
