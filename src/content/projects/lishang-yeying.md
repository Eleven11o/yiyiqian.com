overviewZh: "《里商曳影》是一款基于杭州淳安县非物质文化遗产“里商仁灯”设计的非遗文创玩具。针对传统仁灯制作周期长、工艺复杂、材料获取困难及应用场景单一等问题，项目在保留其典型结构与核心工艺的基础上，对材料、结构和制作流程进行重新设计，将制作时间由约3天缩短至40分钟，主要工序由7步简化为5步。设计以“参与式非遗体验”为核心，将传统节庆花灯转化为可制作、可互动、可进入日常生活的文化产品，使使用者在亲手完成仁灯的过程中理解其结构、工艺与文化价值，探索非遗从“被观看”到“被体验”的当代传承方式。"
overviewEn: "Lishang Yeying is an intangible-cultural-heritage toy inspired by Lishang Rendeng, a traditional lantern craft from Chun'an County, Hangzhou. Responding to the long production cycle, complex techniques, hard-to-source materials, and limited settings of the traditional lantern, the project redesigns its materials, structure, and assembly process while preserving its distinctive form and core craft principles. Production time is reduced from approximately three days to forty minutes, and the main process is simplified from seven steps to five. Centered on participatory heritage experience, the design transforms a festival lantern into a cultural product that can be made, played with, and brought into everyday life. By assembling the lantern themselves, participants encounter its structure, craft, and cultural value firsthand, exploring a contemporary mode of transmission in which intangible heritage shifts from something observed to something experienced."
