<section class="legacy-opening" aria-labelledby="xiaoyaoyou-opening-title">
  <p class="legacy-opening-mark">Archived project · Interactive installation</p>
  <h2 id="xiaoyaoyou-opening-title">逍遥游<span>Xiaoyaoyou · Zhuang Zhou’s Dream</span></h2>
  <div class="legacy-opening-copy">
    <div lang="zh-CN">
      <h3>作品 / Experience</h3>
      <p>《逍遥游》是一件多传感交互机械装置。作品将《千里江山图》的青绿山水与“鲲”的神话意象并置，借由可移动的山峰、云气、机械鲲与投影阴影，讨论庄周梦蝶式的真实与虚幻。</p>
      <p>观众以毛笔、触摸和身体距离介入装置。山水不再是静止画卷，而成为会回应人的机械场景；真实构件和投射影像在同一幅长卷中互相穿越。</p>
    </div>
    <div lang="en">
      <h3>Artwork / Experience</h3>
      <p><em>Xiaoyaoyou</em> is a multi-sensor mechanical installation that brings the blue-green landscape of <em>A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains</em> together with the mythic Kun. Moving peaks, clouds, mechanical fish, and projected shadows explore the ambiguity between reality and illusion associated with Zhuang Zhou’s butterfly dream.</p>
      <p>The audience enters through brush, touch, and bodily distance. Landscape is no longer a static scroll but a responsive mechanical scene in which physical components and projected images pass through one another.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="legacy-chapter legacy-accent-band" data-accent="cyan" aria-labelledby="xiaoyaoyou-concept">
  <header class="legacy-chapter-heading">
    <p class="legacy-part">Concept</p>
    <h2 id="xiaoyaoyou-concept">庄周·梦<span>Zhuang Zhou’s Dream</span></h2>
  </header>

  <blockquote class="legacy-quote" lang="zh-CN">
    昔者庄周梦为胡蝶，栩栩然胡蝶也。
    <cite>《庄子·齐物论》 · Zhuangzi, Discussion on Making All Things Equal</cite>
  </blockquote>

  <div class="legacy-bilingual">
    <div lang="zh-CN">
      <h3>现实与虚幻</h3>
      <p>作品把长卷视为现实与梦境之间的界面：山峰在轨道上平移，云气沿画面游走，鲲从实体机械转化为墙面的光影。观众看到的每一次变化，都同时包含物理运动与视觉幻象。</p>
    </div>
    <div lang="en">
      <h3>Reality and illusion</h3>
      <p>The scroll becomes an interface between waking space and dream. Mountain peaks translate along tracks, clouds travel across the composition, and the Kun shifts from a mechanical body into light and shadow on the wall. Every change combines physical motion with visual illusion.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

<section class="legacy-chapter" aria-labelledby="xiaoyaoyou-question">
  <header class="legacy-chapter-heading">
    <p class="legacy-part">Research question</p>
    <h2 id="xiaoyaoyou-question">触景生境<span>How can a scroll become a responsive environment?</span></h2>
  </header>

  <div class="legacy-bilingual">
    <div lang="zh-CN">
      <h3>从观看到介入</h3>
      <p>当传统长卷被转化为可触发、可移动的空间，观众如何在物理机械与数字投影之间建立连续的观看经验？作品以低门槛的身体动作取代屏幕按钮，使触碰、靠近和移动成为叙事的接口。</p>
    </div>
    <div lang="en">
      <h3>From looking to entering</h3>
      <p>When a traditional scroll becomes a triggerable and mobile environment, how might a viewer form a continuous experience across physical mechanisms and digital projection? The work replaces on-screen buttons with simple bodily actions, making touch, proximity, and movement the interface of the narrative.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="legacy-chapter legacy-accent-band" data-accent="violet" aria-labelledby="xiaoyaoyou-hardware">
  <header class="legacy-chapter-heading">
    <p class="legacy-part">Mechanical system</p>
    <h2 id="xiaoyaoyou-hardware">硬件构造<span>Hardware and spatial choreography</span></h2>
  </header>

  <div class="legacy-bilingual">
    <div lang="zh-CN">
      <h3>山、云与鲲</h3>
      <p>九座山峰沿画面平移，一组云气结构在长卷中移动，三只机械鲲分别旋转。毛笔上的接触传感器控制山峰；两组超声波传感器感知观众距离并控制鲲与云的运动。</p>
      <p>定向光源把机械结构的影子投向卷面，使看得见的实体与无法触碰的投影共同参与构图。</p>
    </div>
    <div lang="en">
      <h3>Mountains, cloud, and Kun</h3>
      <p>Nine mountain peaks translate across the composition, one cloud structure travels along the scroll, and three mechanical Kun rotate. A contact sensor attached to the brush controls the mountains, while two ultrasonic sensors read viewer distance to control the fish and cloud.</p>
      <p>Directional lights cast the mechanisms’ shadows onto the scroll, allowing tangible bodies and untouchable projections to share the same composition.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

<section class="legacy-chapter" aria-labelledby="xiaoyaoyou-software">
  <header class="legacy-chapter-heading">
    <p class="legacy-part">Interaction system</p>
    <h2 id="xiaoyaoyou-software">软件控制<span>Unity, Raspberry Pi, and sensor input</span></h2>
  </header>

  <div class="legacy-bilingual">
    <div lang="zh-CN">
      <h3>交互流程</h3>
      <p>Unity 界面负责组织交互状态，Raspberry Pi 接收传感器信号并与运动控制端通信。测试阶段分别记录了毛笔接触、超声波距离与机械反馈之间的对应关系，再将独立动作合并为完整场景。</p>
    </div>
    <div lang="en">
      <h3>Interaction workflow</h3>
      <p>A Unity interface organized the interaction states, while a Raspberry Pi received sensor input and communicated with the motion-control system. Brush contact, ultrasonic distance, and mechanical feedback were tested separately before the actions were assembled into the complete scene.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
