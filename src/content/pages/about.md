biography: "My research focuses on immersive experiences, extended reality (XR), and human–computer interaction (HCI). I am currently pursuing an M.A. in Digital Media Art at China Academy of Art and am a member of its AI Center. I explore embodied experience, spatial narrative, and user experience in virtual reality, with particular attention to contemporary expressions of digital cultural heritage. My practice spans cinematic VR and interactive installations, connecting artistic creation with research questions, design methods, and experience evaluation. Recent projects include the VR films Deepcore: When Cave Breath and The Enigma of the Divine Capital, and the collective experience space Resonance. My research has been published at ACM ICHEC 2025, SIGGRAPH Asia 2025 Posters, and VINCI 2026. I received the 2024 National Scholarship for Graduate Students, graduated from China Academy of Art in the top 5% of my undergraduate cohort, and was named an Outstanding Graduate of Zhejiang Province."
